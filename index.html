Family snow trip · 2 adults, 2 kids, 5 nights from Houston

Christmas week or February?

You are both right. February is cheaper everywhere we looked. But the expensive week is the one after Christmas, and in Steamboat Springs the week before it costs $396 more than February.

  • 0 vs 5School days missed: Christmas break against a February week (Conroe ISD calendar)

Every price is a live quote read on November 14, 2026. Prices will move; the gaps between weeks are the point.

The recommendation

Steamboat Springs, Dec 21–26, home on the 26th. About $3,490 for flights, hotel and car, against $3,094 in late February. The $396 difference buys a white Christmas with a 99% snow record and no missed school.

If the $396 is better spent elsewhere: the same trip on Feb 22–27 has 10 more inches of snow on the ground and warmer afternoons, and costs 5 school days.

Not recommended in any plan: any stay that runs past Dec 26, and Presidents Day week (Feb 12–17), which prices like Christmas.

And the February week without missing school

Conroe ISD is off Friday Feb 12 and Monday Feb 15. That long weekend is Presidents Day, and it prices like Christmas: the same 11 Steamboat hotels averaged $396 a night for Feb 12–17 against $339 for Dec 21–26, and the nonstop flight is $546 against $389.

#1 · Colorado

Steamboat Springs

Best overall. Lowest sleeping elevation of the Colorado ski towns, a 99% snow record, a nonstop flight both ways, and a Christmas markup under $400.

6,695 ft. Under the CDC's 8,000 ft caution line.

Getting there. Nonstop from Houston, 3 hr 8 min, then a 28-minute drive.

What makes it special. A wide, snowy ranch valley with the Yampa River running through town and hot springs steaming in the cold.

  • Snow tubing at Saddleback Ranch. $50.88 per rider for 2 hours; age 4 and under free on a parent's tube.
  • Old Town Hot Springs pools. $27 per child (3–14), $35 per adult. Closed Christmas Day.
  • Winter Carnival, Feb 2–7, 2027. Street events with horses pulling kids on skis. February only (date from the GPT lane's source).

February uses Feb 22–27 here because Feb 1–6 is Winter Carnival week, when the same hotels cost as much as Christmas.

6,237 ft. Under the CDC's 8,000 ft caution line.

Getting there. Nonstop from Houston on Dec 21 (3 hr 35 min); one stop on the February dates. Town is 15 minutes from the airport.

What makes it special. The Teton range rising straight up from the valley, and thousands of wild elk wintering beside town.

Snow record is from Moose, inside Grand Teton National Park, 12 miles north. The town of Jackson holds less snow.

Limits

  • Hotel comparisons use 6 to 11 hotels per town: the ones Booking.com listed first in both weeks with a Christmas rate under $700 a night. Small samples; treat a gap under $200 as a tie.
  • Rates are before lodging tax and resort fees. Flights exclude bags.
  • Activity prices other than the three spot-checked ones come from the pages the GPT lane cited.
  • Rental cars were priced for Christmas week and February only, not for Dec 26–31 outside Denver.
  • Snow records describe 1991–2020. Colorado's early season was thin in December 2025.
  • The school calendar is Conroe ISD's. Other districts differ.
  • The altitude guidance is the CDC's general advice for travelers, not medical advice for your children.
