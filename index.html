Family snow trip · 2 adults, 2 kids, 5 nights from Houston

Where and when for the snow trip?

Two decisions: which week, and which place. The short answer is the week before Christmas, Dec 16–21, in Whitefish or Duluth. The one option that puts you in the snow on Christmas morning for a fair price is Steamboat Springs.

1. The three options 2. Which week 3. Which place 4. All nine, ranked 5. The detail

Every price is a live quote read on November 14, 2026, for flights, hotel and rental car for four people and five nights. Food and activities are left out because they cost the same in any week.

2. Which week

Dec 16–21

the week before

Cost. Cheaper than Christmas week in all 9 places, and within $200 of February in 6 of them. Hotels in the mountain towns run 12% to 34% under Christmas week.

Snow. The four snow-sure mountain towns 95% to 98%. Whitefish 84%, Duluth 87%. About 3 inches thinner than Christmas Day.

What is different. Home for Christmas morning. Elk sleigh rides start Dec 19 and Quebec's snow park opens Dec 18, so the end of the stay catches both.

Dec 21–26

Christmas week

Cost. Flights to Denver and Steamboat cost about the same as February. Flights to Whitefish, Duluth and Quebec City rise 31% to 81% over the week before.

Snow. Best December odds: 98% to 100% in four mountain towns, 92% in Whitefish and Duluth.

What is different. The only week you wake up there on Dec 25. Steamboat's hot springs and Jackson's elk sleigh rides are closed that day.

Dec 26–31

the real peak

Cost. Avoid. The Steamboat nonstop goes from $389 to $1,080 a person and mountain hotels rise 7% to 51%.

Snow. Same as Christmas week.

What is different. Fewest rooms left of any week.

February

a non-holiday week

Cost. Cheapest week in 5 of 9 places, usually by less than $200. Red River is the exception: $1,191 against $2,651.

Snow. The deepest of the winter: about 60% more on the ground than at Christmas in the snow-sure towns, and 98% to 100% everywhere but Estes Park and Red River.

What is different. No Christmas events. Skip Feb 12–17. In Steamboat use Feb 22–27, after Winter Carnival.

  • The low places are cheap the week before Christmas and expensive in Christmas week, because their flights jump. Whitefish costs $1,456 more in Christmas week.
  • The Colorado towns are the reverse on flights: the Denver fare is $341 to $361 in all three weeks. Their gap is hotels and the rental car ($435 at Denver in Christmas week, $181 to $191 otherwise).
  • Elevation and snow certainty pull in opposite directions. The places above 6,000 ft have the 95%-plus snow records, except Estes Park and Red River. The low places are 84% to 92% in December.

Tubing hills and height

Rules found for a child between 36 and 42 inches tall:

  • Can ride: Spirit Mountain in Duluth (ages 3 and up), Saddleback Ranch in Steamboat (age 4 and under shares a parent's tube), Winter Park's Coca-Cola Tube Hill (36 inches and up).
  • Cannot ride under 42 inches: Snow King in Jackson, Red River, Mount Norquay in Banff.
  • No height rule: the elk sleigh ride in Jackson, and the sleigh and dog sled rides near Whitefish.
  • Opening dates: none of the mountain tubing hills has published one for this season. Saddleback Ranch says a bookable slot online means it is open. Snow King opens when there is enough snow; its page shows Dec 20 last season.

Also suggested by the AI researchers and not priced: Banff (4,540 ft, needs passports), West Yellowstone, Tahoe City, and Santa Fe (ruled out: 9% chance of snow in town at Christmas).

Limits of this comparison
  • Hotel comparisons use 6 to 13 hotels per town: the ones Booking.com listed first in each week being compared. Small samples; treat a gap under $200 as a tie.
  • For the six mountain towns, the Dec 16–21 hotel rate is the Christmas-week rate multiplied by the price ratio at hotels listed in both weeks. Breckenridge's ratio rests on 6 hotels.
  • Rates are before lodging tax and resort fees. Flights exclude bags.
  • Prices marked "older price" are from past seasons. Activity prices other than the three spot-checked ones come from pages the AI lanes cited or from tourism sites.
  • Snow records describe 1991–2020. Colorado's early season was thin in December 2025.
  • The oxygen figures are standard-atmosphere pressure at each town's elevation compared with sea level.
  • The altitude guidance is the CDC's general advice for travelers, not medical advice for your children.
