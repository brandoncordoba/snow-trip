# Snow trip: Christmas week or February?

A one-page comparison of six snow destinations from Houston for a family with
kids aged 4 to 7: what Christmas week costs against a February week, how sure
the snow is, and what else differs.

- **Page:** `index.html`, built by `python3 build.py` from the numbers at the top of that file.
- **Evidence:** `research/` holds the raw price observations (Booking.com, Google Flights, Kayak), the NOAA station normals, and the AI research output that was cross-checked.
- **Design:** type scale, spacing and color tokens come from the site-starter stylesheet.

All prices were read on 2026-11-14 and will have moved since.
